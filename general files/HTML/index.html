About MUSE

MUSE is an online coach for anyone and everyone who loves dance. It remembers yesterday's performance, enhances today's and prepares you for tomorrow's.

MUSE compares your movement to the choreography you are aiming to learn so you can improve your accuracy. With performance stats being recoreded, you can easily look back and see how far you've come as a dancer.

  • 01

    ✦ Synchronization Analyzer

    Align your routines effortlessly using video editing tools and an integrated metronome to refine your timing down to the exact beat.

  • 02

    ✦ Media Gallery

    Keep your dance videos organized in one place with custom filters and search options, making it fast and easy to navigate your library.

  • 03

    ✦ Growth & Progress Statistics

    Track your skill development over time through detailed visual stats that highlight your consistency, improvement, and milestones.
